## Rules for any future book
1. Do not sell what is already free online. At least half of the pages must be material that is not on the blog: unpublished stories, a route map or timetable per place, a "Getting There" section, a printable checklist. The free blog posts stay free (they bring search traffic).
2. Reuse of Book 1 stories: do not repeat a story that is already in Book 1. Check the Book 1 manuscript (Notion page "Book1原稿") before using a story.
3. No real names, employer or day job (same as the blog rules). Guests by country only.
4. Only real experiences. Never invent.
